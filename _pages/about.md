I am a Ph.D. student in Information Engineering and Computer Science at the **University of Trento**, Italy, supervised by **Prof. Elisa Ricci** and **Dr. Massimiliano Mancini**.

My research focuses on **multimodal large language models** and **LLM post-training**. I study proactiveness in multimodal LLMs and how post-training shapes factual knowledge, including its acquisition, loss, and reappearance. My earlier work explored **continual learning** and **machine unlearning**, with an emphasis on updating classification models and forgetting outdated or private information.

From January to June 2026, I was a visiting researcher at **FunAI Lab**, working with **Prof. Yuki Asano** in Nuremberg on factual knowledge during LLM post-training. Previously, I was a research intern with **Inria's Thoth team** in Grenoble and an AI engineer intern at **BlueTensor** in Trento.

I have hands-on experience training supervised and self-supervised vision encoders, fine-tuning multimodal LLMs, and post-training LLMs with reinforcement learning and distillation, including GRPO and on-policy self-distillation. I also work with multi-node distributed training and am interested in deep learning architectures, model efficiency, and video LLMs.
