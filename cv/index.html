Summary I am a Ph.D. student at the University of Trento, supervised by Prof. Elisa Ricci and Dr. Massimiliano Mancini. My research focuses on multimodal large language models, proactiveness, and how post-training shapes factual knowledge, building on earlier work in continual learning and machine unlearning.
